DIDSAGO Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

DIDSAGO works on your device without requiring a DIDSAGO account.

Data on your device

Items, dates, reminder settings, history, notes, preferences and optional photos are stored locally. DIDSAGO does not use advertising trackers, sell personal information or receive your items as part of its normal operation.

Optional iCloud sync for PRO

iCloud sync is off by default. If you enable it with DIDSAGO PRO, item details, history and photos are stored in the private CloudKit database of your Apple iCloud account so they can appear on your other devices. We do not operate or have access to this private database. Sync requires an iCloud account, storage and network access. Changes can take time to appear on another device. Turning sync off stops transfers but does not remove copies already in iCloud.

Photos and camera

DIDSAGO accesses your camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo. Selected photos are stored locally and, if you enable iCloud sync, in your private iCloud database.

Voice input

If you tap the microphone, DIDSAGO requests microphone and speech-recognition access. Apple may process audio and transcription under its privacy terms. DIDSAGO uses the returned text to fill the item name and does not store the audio.

Notifications

DIDSAGO schedules local reminders on your device when enabled. You can choose an alert at the due time and an optional early reminder. Item data is not sent to a remote notification server.

In-app purchases

Apple processes optional DIDSAGO PRO purchases and subscriptions through the App Store. DIDSAGO reads the purchase entitlement to unlock PRO features but does not receive your payment card details.

Exporting and deleting data

You can export local DIDSAGO data from Settings. If you have never enabled iCloud sync, you can also delete all local items, history, notes and photos there. After enabling sync, you can delete individual items, but the app cannot currently delete all synced data at once. Deleting the app may remove local data but does not erase iCloud copies. You choose where to store or share an exported file.

Children

DIDSAGO is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or privacy practices change. The current version is available in the app and on this page.
