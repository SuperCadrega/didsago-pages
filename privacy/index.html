DIDSAGO Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

DIDSAGO is designed to work primarily on your device and does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

Items, dates, reminder settings, notification preferences, history, notes, custom icons, optional photos and app preferences are stored locally on your device. DIDSAGO does not use advertising trackers and does not sell personal information.

Photos and camera

DIDSAGO accesses the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to an item. Selected photos are stored locally for use inside the app.

Voice input

If you tap the microphone, DIDSAGO requests microphone and speech-recognition access. Your audio and transcription may be processed by Apple’s speech-recognition services under Apple’s privacy terms. DIDSAGO uses the returned text only to fill the item name and does not store the audio.

Notifications

DIDSAGO schedules local notifications on your device when you enable reminders. For each item you can choose one notification at the due time or an additional early reminder calculated from the selected interval. Item data is not sent to a remote notification server.

In-app purchases

Optional DIDSAGO PRO purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. DIDSAGO may read the entitlement supplied by Apple to unlock PRO features, but does not receive or store your payment card details.

Exporting and deleting data

You can export your local data or permanently delete items, history, notes and photos from Settings. You decide where an exported file is stored or shared.

Children

DIDSAGO is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically directed to children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when DIDSAGO features or privacy practices change. The current version is available in the app and on this page.
