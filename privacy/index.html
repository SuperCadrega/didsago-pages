DIDSAGO Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

DIDSAGO is designed to help users remember recurring activities and keep track of when they were last completed.

Data collection

DIDSAGO does not use advertising trackers and does not sell personal information. Information you create in the app is used only to provide the app’s functionality.

Photos

DIDSAGO accesses the camera or photo library only when you choose to add a photo to an item. Access is subject to the permissions you grant on your device.

Notifications

DIDSAGO may use local notifications to provide reminders you configure. Notification permission can be changed in iOS Settings.

In-app purchases

Optional DIDSAGO PRO purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. DIDSAGO does not receive or store your payment card details.

Children

DIDSAGO is not specifically directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when DIDSAGO features or privacy practices change.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact didsago.support@gmail.com.
